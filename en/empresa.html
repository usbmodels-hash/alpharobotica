Who we are

Alpha Robotics: specialist integrator of KEENON robots for hotels and hospitality in Spain.

We turn KEENON service robotics into real operating projects: workflow assessment, measurable pilot, deployment, team training and local technical support.

What we do

KEENON robots applied to cleaning, dining, room service and internal logistics.

We work with hotels, restaurants, hospitals, senior living, supermarkets and operations with internal logistics. We select the robot and accessories for each use case and integrate them into the existing operation.

Autonomous cleaning

KLEENBOT C30, C40 and C55 for scrubbing and sweeping common areas, corridors and large floors. See autonomous cleaning →

Food & Beverage

DINERBOT T8, T9, T10, T11 and T3 for table service, buffets and closed deliveries. See Food & Beverage →

Room service

BUTLERBOT W3 for in-room deliveries and amenities, with elevator integration when the project requires it. See room service →

Internal logistics

KEENON S100 and S300 (Heavy Load range) for moving loads, stores and luggage. See internal logistics →

Alpha method

How we work: assessment, measurable pilot, deployment and support.

Before discussing models or fees, we analyse routes, schedules, elevators, floor areas, congestion points and operating cost. We plan the demonstration and the go-live according to the property and the integrations required.

  1. 01Assessment

    Map of areas, routes, shifts and hidden costs.

  2. 02Pilot

    Validation of one specific area with clear metrics and goals.

  3. 03Deployment

    Routes, maps, schedules, accessories, training and protocols.

  4. 04Support

    Performance follow-up, incident handling and return review with Alpha Care.

Manufacturer and partner

KEENON Robotics builds the robots; Alpha Robotics deploys and supports them in Spain.

KEENON Robotics manufactures the KLEENBOT, DINERBOT, BUTLERBOT and Heavy Load ranges. Alpha Robotics is its official integrator in Spain: we design the solution, configure the fleet, train the team and keep the operation running.

The international KEENON references shown on this website are the manufacturer's projects. Our contribution is local deployment and hands-on support for every client in Spain.
