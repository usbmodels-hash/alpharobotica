How to choose a professional cleaning robot for your facility

Start with the floor area that needs cleaning each day, the type of dirt and the time available to complete each route. Separate dry vacuuming and maintenance tasks from work that requires an autonomous scrubber for hard floors.

Dry vacuuming or hard-floor scrubbing

The KLEENBOT C30 combines sweeping, vacuuming and dry mopping. The KLEENBOT C40 adds scrubbing, with a 16 L clean-water tank and an 11 L waste-water tank. The KLEENBOT C55 is an option to assess for higher-volume scrubbing operations. Where carpet is present, check compatibility and specify a dry-cleaning route. Wet scrubbing is reserved for compatible hard floors.

Daily workload and access requirements

Measure the usable floor area and how often each zone needs cleaning. A lobby that requires several passes creates a different workload from a corridor cleaned once a day. Check narrow passages, turns, slopes, doors and space for the docking station. Published performance in m²/h provides a technical reference; practical performance must be validated at the facility.

Hotels, care homes and facilities that stay open

Hotels need to coordinate lobbies, corridors and meeting rooms with guest arrivals and departures. Care homes need routes and schedules that fit residents' routines and staff activity. Offices and retail premises must account for opening hours and changes to furniture layouts. The robot becomes part of the facility's cleaning plan, including supervision, consumables checks and incident handling.

What to check before a demonstration

  • A floor plan or list of zones, floor materials and daily cleaning area.
  • Cleaning frequency, permitted operating hours and pedestrian traffic.
  • Dock location and water, drainage and power requirements if an automatic station is proposed.
  • Lifts and doors that require integration, subject to a technical assessment.
  • Supervision time and the cleaning standard used to evaluate the pilot.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about autonomous cleaning.

Which KEENON cleaning robot fits a hotel best?

The C30 suits compact areas, the C40 is usually the most balanced model for ROI, and the C55 is better for large surfaces or intensive use.

Can the return be calculated before buying?

Yes. Alpha Robotics can estimate freed-up hours, cost per m², cleaning frequency and payback with an indicative ROI calculator.
