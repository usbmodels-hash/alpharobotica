Capabilities, requirements and limitations

  • F&B T range: payload up to 40 kg depending on the model for floor service, buffet, clearing and kitchen-to-floor support.
  • Integration/connectivity: W3, S100 and the C range certified for multi-floor integration; autonomous use of lifts and automatic doors via a dedicated network and IoT (LoRaWAN/radio frequency), without interfering with guest WiFi.

Maintenance and SLA

  • Permanent technical support, parts, labour, technician call-outs and consumables included in the OPEX model.
  • In the event of a critical breakdown that compromises operations: replacement of the robot with a new or courtesy unit at no additional cost.

Frequently asked questions about the KEENON T8

The questions we get before deploying a KEENON T8. If you can't find yours, message us on WhatsApp.

Which restaurants is the KEENON T8 waiter robot suitable for?

The T8 is a compact option for restaurants, cafés, hotels and dining rooms with narrow aisles. It carries trays of plates and drinks, cuts repetitive trips and frees staff up for customer service. Passage width is checked during the assessment.

Does the T8 replace waiters?

No. The T8 provides operational support: it moves trays and cuts repetitive trips so staff can spend more time on customer service, sales and the dining experience.

What is analysed before deploying a T8?

Alpha Robotics reviews kitchen-to-floor distances, aisle width, the number of tables, shifts, plate volume and time-saving goals to size the solution.

Can I compare the T8 with the T9 or the T10?

Yes. The assessment compares the T8, T9, T10 and T11 by capacity, visual presence, dining-room size, type of service and expected return.

How much does the KEENON T8 waiter robot cost?

The price is on request. Available to buy or as RaaS / OpEx Flow with a monthly fee. Request a free demo with estimated ROI.
