CAPEX vs OPEX/RaaS

KEENON robot acquisition models: CAPEX, finance leasing and OpExFlow.

The commercial decision should be kept separate from the technical one. Outright purchase, financing or a monthly-fee service, with ownership, maintenance and continuity conditions depending on the contracted option. You can buy the robot outright, finance it as a CAPEX asset or take out an OpExFlow service with no ownership and the support defined in the contract.

See the CAPEX optionSee the OpExFlow option

Clear comparison

Don't mix finance leasing with OpExFlow.

Both routes can be paid monthly, but they follow different logics in accounting, operational and contractual terms.

Practical decision

Choose by scale, operational risk and in-house capacity.

The technology can be the same; what changes is how you take on investment, maintenance, continuity and operational responsibility.

CAPEX / ownership

Food service and local businesses.

Outright purchase, financial renting or leasing; ownership conditions at the end depend on the financing contract. Recommended when the business wants to control the asset and start with a contained rollout.

See the purchase route (CAPEX)

OpExFlow / service

Hotels and large accounts.

€0 upfront, a monthly fee, SLA, consumables included and replacement on critical failure. Recommended when continuity matters more than ownership.

See the service route (Enterprise)

Indicative packs by hotel size.
